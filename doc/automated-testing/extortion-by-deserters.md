# Extortion by Deserters

Live testing is pending. Use the lane assigned by the supervisor, with a dedicated server and two clients. All state-changing setup and world actions originate on the server; clients perform their own dialogue and read-only inspection. Do not assign battle winners or completed quest states as gameplay evidence.

Use Polisia (`village_ES1_2`), Tegresos (`village_ES1_3`) and Erebulos (`village_ES1_4`), bound to Danustica (`town_ES1`). Find the current save's headmen and player identities with `coop.debug.issues.list_types`, `coop.debug.town.list_towns` and the existing issue setup/inspection commands before choosing the givers. The quest-specific eligibility is vanilla's headman/village offer, town security, relation, war and troop checks. The per-player duplicate check belongs to the framework.

For every flow, compare the server and both clients' party identities, gold, clan renown, giver relation, quest state and journal. Personal progress belongs to the accepter; shared town security/prosperity and giver power change once on the server. Honor XP follows the framework's current suppression of the shared trait store. Capture the named parties and relevant dialogue/journal on both clients before and after each decisive result.

The three reward choices use the promised payment shown in dialogue. Full payment grants 100% gold, 0 renown, +4 giver relation, +5 giver power and +10 town security. Half payment grants 50% gold, +1 renown, +6 relation, +10 power, +10 security and +25 prosperity. Refusing payment grants 0 gold, +2 renown, +8 relation, +15 power, +10 security and +100 prosperity. These are vanilla base values before other active game modifiers. The installed issue offers no lord solution.

1. Offer, decline and accept through real village dialogue and campaign-map quick talk. Check security above/below the offer/stay-alive limits, relation below -10, war and fewer than twenty healthy troops. Decline leaves no quest or deserter party; a valid accept creates one registered server deserter party and an owner-only journal.
2. Have both clients take separate deserter issues from different headmen concurrently. Each gets its own world party and personal journal. A second deserter issue for the same player is refused by the framework. Race both players for the same issue and verify one owner and one party.
3. Wait in the village for the deserter raid. The owner gets the ambush conversation and real supported encounter/mission, with one server-created defender party. The other client does not get the owner's encounter prompt or journal. Win the battle, return to the giver and separately choose full payment, half payment and no payment. Verify vanilla's distinct gold, renown, relation and town effects, and completed owner journal.
4. Fight the deserters outside the village. Verify defeat progress from a real victory, then hand in a reward. Let the other player defeat a quest's deserters and verify cancellation without a stranded quest.
5. Lose the battle. The deserters resume raiding and temporary defenders are cleaned up after the encounter. Leave during the raid, allow deserters to ravage the village, raid it as the owner, and have an unrelated party raid it. Verify the distinct failure/cancellation logs and penalties.
6. Approach the deserters outside the village so they flee. Exercise staying in pursuit, losing sight and the ten-hour escape deadline separately. Verify failure, correct party release/destruction and an unchanged second player's personal state.
7. Let the twelve-day quest deadline expire. Declare war, change the owner's clan/kingdom so the giver becomes hostile, attack a village party, and exercise inherited giver death/captivity/cancellation conditions where vanilla exposes them. Verify each applicable cancellation/failure and cleanup once.
8. Choose the companion solution through dialogue and the troop picker. Check invalid tier/count and companion skill choices, then success and failure, casualties and experience, normal troop return and deferred return while the owner cannot receive troops. Only the owning player gets the returned troops, payment and personal log.
9. Save/reload and reconnect while waiting, fleeing, after battle victory and during a companion mission. Verify restored ownership, party binding, journal visibility and no duplicate event consequences or parties.

The retained historical branch matrix under ignored `artifacts/IssueToPr` is source research, not proof that any of these flows passed. Record unavailable branches as unverified.
